FIVE FRONTS. One CREW.

Start with what you need. The rest is there when the budget is. Projects from €350. Retainers from €600/month.

01 The Engine Monthly content that keeps you in the feed. €600 per month

For brands and artists who need to show up consistently without handing over their whole identity. We plan, shoot, edit and caption. You keep the vision.

Start the engine
  • 8 vertical videos per month (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)
  • 2 edited photo sets
  • Scripts, hooks and captions
  • Monthly content plan and posting guidance
  • 1 revision round per asset
02 The Launch One campaign, fully loaded. €750 per launch

For a drop, a release, an opening or a rebrand. One production day, one clear story, every format you need to push it.

Plan a launch
  • 1 hero film (60–90 seconds)
  • 6 vertical cutdowns
  • 10 edited campaign photos
  • Launch copy for feed, story and email
  • 2 revision rounds
03 The Identity A brand that looks like it means it. €900 per identity

For new brands and artists who need more than a logo. A complete visual system that holds up on a hoodie, a poster and a phone screen.

Build an identity
  • Logo suite: primary, secondary and mark
  • Color palette and typography
  • Brand guide (PDF)
  • 6 social templates
  • 2 revision rounds
04 The Press Kit One folder that opens doors. €350 per kit

For artists, athletes and brands who need to look professional to venues, bookers, sponsors and media. Everything in one place, ready to send.

Build a press kit
  • Bio and positioning copy
  • 5–10 edited photos
  • Cover art and selected tracks
  • Press highlights and milestones
  • PDF + web page version
05 The Recap Chaos, captured live. €500 per event

For events, shows and activations. We capture the room and turn it into social proof while the energy is still fresh.

Cover an event
  • Up to 4 hours of event coverage
  • 1 recap film (60–90 seconds)
  • 10 vertical cutdowns
  • 20 edited photos
  • Delivery within 5–7 days

Fashion production, music, publishing and full event activation: on request.
